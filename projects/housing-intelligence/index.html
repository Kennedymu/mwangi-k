Selected Build / Data & Decision Intelligence

Affordable Housing Demand & Supply Intelligence

A data analytics and decision-support platform designed to map housing demand, track housing supply, transform programme data into management dashboards and support evidence-informed planning decisions.

Data Analytics Platform Housing Demand Housing Supply Decision Support
01 / Context

Turning housing data into planning intelligence

Affordable housing programmes generate information about demand, planned and available supply, projects and programme delivery. The value of that information depends on whether decision-makers can organize, analyze and interpret it.

Affordable Housing Demand & Supply Intelligence was developed to create a structured analytics layer between programme data and management decision-making.

02 / Decision Problem

Connecting demand, supply and management visibility

The platform addresses the need to move beyond isolated datasets and reporting outputs toward a clearer view of housing demand and supply for programme planning.

Understand Demand

Organize housing demand information so that patterns can be interpreted and presented to programme decision-makers.

Track Supply

Structure housing supply information to improve visibility of programme delivery and available or planned supply.

Connect the Two

Bring demand and supply perspectives into a common analytical environment rather than treating them as disconnected reporting streams.

Management Visibility

Translate underlying information into dashboards and management views that support planning.

03 / Solution

From programme data to decision support

The solution structures the analytics workflow from underlying programme information through analysis and visualization to management decision support.

06 / Analytics Approach

Analytics designed around the decision

The platform is designed as a data analytics and decision-support system. The objective is not simply to create dashboards, but to structure information around the questions programme teams need to answer.

This means working backward from management and planning requirements: identifying the information required, structuring the data, applying the appropriate analysis and then presenting the result in a form that supports interpretation and action.
